Evaluation: the OLD test split (rows 2128–2255; previously inspected, so exploratory). Lower is better. B = 128 = the reused matched runs.

**Decision (prespecified rule): LOW-BUDGET-COVERAGE-NO-GAIN**

Valid runs: 48 / 48 (one evaluation universe of 240 experts).
